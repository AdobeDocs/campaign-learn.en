# Technical Tutorial - Set up SMS for Adobe Campaign

The tutorials in this section have been designed for administrators, who are tasked with setting up the SMS channel for Adobe Campaign.

The following topics are covered:

* **[Introduction to SMS](/help/tutorial-sms/introduction-to-sms.md)**:
 *Learn how SMS works and how Adobe Campaign sends SMS*

* **[Set up an SMS account for a standard SMPP provider](/help/tutorial-sms/set-up-account-for-standard-smpp-provider.md)**
*Learn how to adapt the SMS connector to your SMPP provider. Fine tune your SMS settings to handle connection limits.  Understand how to set up the maximum throughput, the sending window, and encryption with TLS.*

* **[Adapt the SMS connector to your SMPP provider](/help/tutorial-sms/adapt-sms-connector-to-smpp-provider.md)**
  *Learn how to fine tune your SMS settings to handle connection limits. Understand how to set up the maximum throughput, a sending window, and encryption with TLS.*
  
* **[SMPP protocol deep dive and troubleshooting](/help/tutorial-sms/smpp-deep-dive-and-troubleshooting.md)**
  *Learn how SMPP connections are established and how SMPP exchanges data through PDUs. Understand how to troubleshoot connections.*

>[!NOTE]
>
>This tutorial applies to Adobe Campaign V7 and Campaign V8. Additional resources can be found in the product documentation: [SMS connector protocol and settings](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/campaign-classic/using/sending-messages/sending-messages-on-mobiles/sms-set-up/sms-protocol).
